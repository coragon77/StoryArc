# Re-plan keeps the situation and replaces the road

An arc has two layers with different lifetimes. The situation (premise, secrets, fulfilled beats, planted foreshadowing, fired triggers) stays true whatever the player does; the road (unfulfilled beats, unplanted foreshadowing, unfired triggers, and the guidance written for them: directives, anti-patterns, tone, themes, escalation, estimated length, tier) is one predicted path and goes stale when the story diverges. Re-plan therefore never regenerates secrets (the model may only add), keeps every fulfilled item with its id, and replaces the rest from a fresh three-phase generation that sees the current chat. Title and premise are player-edited, never model-regenerated. We rejected letting the model judge secrets as "contradicted": it requires the blind player to review a diff, and a contradicted secret is harmless when simply never revealed.

## Amendment 2026-10-25: re-plan may grow the tier, never shrink it

Re-plan offers the arc's own tier or a larger one: an episode can become an act or saga, an act a saga, a saga stays a saga. Shrinking was possible before and is now refused in the wizard (`replanTiers` in `replan.js`). The kept situation is the reason. A saga's fulfilled milestones are states the story reached in any order; an episode's beats are ordered scenes with one active at a time and a pacing clock, so kept milestones have no meaning inside an episode. Growing works the other way round: completed scene beats stay completed and print nothing in the milestone section, and the new milestones are all active. A story that needs a scene-level episode inside a saga gets a new episode arc next to the saga. It also keeps background focus (ADR-0001 amendment) on acts and sagas only, without a rule for what a background arc becomes as an episode.
