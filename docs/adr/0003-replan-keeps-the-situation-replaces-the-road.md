# Re-plan keeps the situation and replaces the road

An arc has two layers with different lifetimes. The situation (premise, secrets, fulfilled beats, planted foreshadowing, fired triggers) stays true whatever the player does; the road (unfulfilled beats, unplanted foreshadowing, unfired triggers, and the guidance written for them: directives, anti-patterns, tone, themes, escalation, estimated length, tier) is one predicted path and goes stale when the story diverges. Re-plan therefore never regenerates secrets (the model may only add), keeps every fulfilled item with its id, and replaces the rest from a fresh three-phase generation that sees the current chat. Title and premise are player-edited, never model-regenerated. We rejected letting the model judge secrets as "contradicted": it requires the blind player to review a diff, and a contradicted secret is harmless when simply never revealed.
